24h turbo search

We wanted to do the “quadrillion games in a day” thing – and, admittedly, we were also a bit impatient. So we rented eight NVIDIA RTX 5090 GPUs and had them play about 1.00 × 1015 games between 7 Nov 2026, 00:12 and 8 Nov 2026, 00:38, at a rate of 11.4 billion games per second. The longest game they found has 7696 cards.

That is clearly a disappointment, because our own record is longer and was found after only about 1.79 × 1014 games. In hindsight, that record was a stroke of luck: the chance of finding such a game so early is only about 8 %. The turbo run, on the other hand, was somewhat unlucky, since the probability of finding nothing longer than 7696 cards in 1015 games is only about 18 %. (Then again, the chance of beating our record in this run was only about 36 % anyway.)
